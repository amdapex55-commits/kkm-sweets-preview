A karkhana, not a factory.

KKM has stood on Daraya Lal Street in Jodia Bazar since 1948. The same family, the same copper pans, the same Memon recipes carried down by people rather than paper.

Nothing here is made ahead. The halwai starts before dawn, the trays go out to the counter by ten, and whatever is left at closing does not come back the next day.

  • 01Ingredients bought whole and ground in-house — no pre-mix.
  • 02Small batches, cooked to colour and smell, not to a timer.
  • 03Sealed and boxed by hand, the same morning it is sold.
  • 04Karachi same day; other cities in two to three working days.
